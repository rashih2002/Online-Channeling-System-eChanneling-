 Online Channeling System (eChanneling) – BPMN Process Modeling Project
 Project Overview

The Online Channeling System (eChanneling) is a healthcare appointment management platform that enables patients to schedule doctor consultations online, manage appointments, make payments, receive notifications, and complete consultations efficiently.

This project was developed as part of the IS203.3 – Business Process Management module at NSBM Green University. The objective was to analyze an existing healthcare appointment process, identify process inefficiencies, design an improved business process, and model the workflow using Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN 2.0).

Project Objectives
Analyze the existing appointment booking process.
Identify process gaps and inefficiencies.
Design an improved ("To-Be") business process.
Create a BPMN 2.0 process model.
Improve patient experience through automation.
Reduce scheduling conflicts and appointment failures.
Enhance communication between patients, doctors, and hospital staff.

Business Process Scope
Process Boundary
The process begins when a patient accesses the online channeling platform to book an appointment and ends when the consultation is completed and payment has been finalized.

Process Improvement Analysis
The project examined the traditional appointment booking workflow and identified several challenges:

Identified Issues
Appointment cancellations not handled efficiently.
Lack of proactive notifications.
No alternative scheduling mechanism.
Limited communication between stakeholders.
Potential patient waiting times due to doctor unavailability.

Implemented Improvements
Automated SMS and Email notifications.
Appointment reminder functionality.
Alternative time-slot recommendation process.
Cancellation management workflow.
Refund/Cashback handling for canceled consultations.
Enhanced doctor and patient availability verification.
